# Choices Explained

## Mechatronic System Options

Your groups may select from the following options. There's a bit of information on each to get you started.

!!! warning "Please Note:"

    The following just gives you examples of what you **could** look into for each topic. You still need to **answer the questions on the previous page, in response to** [**Directional Verbs**](directional-verbs.md)**.**


***

**Robotic Surgical Systems**:\
Explore the mechatronic systems used in robotic-assisted surgery, such as the **da Vinci Surgical System**. Investigate their impact on **minimally invasive procedures, patient outcomes,** and the **evolution of surgical techniques**.

***

**Autonomous Vehicles**:\
Research mechatronic systems in **self-driving cars** and their impact on **transportation, safety, and urban planning**. Analyze how these systems have evolved and the **functional requirements** for autonomous driving.

***

**Industrial Automation and Robotics**:\
Examine mechatronic systems used in **manufacturing automation**, including **robotic arms** and **conveyor systems**. Investigate their role in **improving production efficiency** and **reducing human labor**.

***

**Smart Home Automation**:\
Explore mechatronic systems in **smart homes**, such as **home security systems, intelligent thermostats,** and **voice-controlled assistants**. Assess their impact on **convenience, energy efficiency,** and **security**.

***

**Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (Drones)**:\
Investigate mechatronic systems used in **drones**, their applications in various industries (e.g., **agriculture, surveillance, delivery**), and the evolving **regulations and privacy concerns** surrounding drone use.

***

**Assistive Technologies**:\
Research mechatronic systems designed to **assist individuals with disabilities**, such as **exoskeletons, mobility aids,** and **communication devices**.

***

**Agricultural Automation**

Explore the **mechatronic systems** used in **modern agriculture**, such as **autonomous tractors, robotic harvesters, precision irrigation systems,** and **AI-driven crop monitoring**. Investigate their impact on **efficiency, sustainability, and food production** while considering the **challenges of technology adoption in farming**.
