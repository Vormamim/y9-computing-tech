# Evaluation

!!! info "Learning Intention"

    * Evaluate their own project and that of their peers using predetermined functional and non-functional requirements
    * Propose software and hardware modifications to increase the effectiveness of a mechatronic and/or automated system



!!! success "Success Criteria"

    * I can evaluate my own project and my peers' projects using predetermined functional and non-functional requirements to identify strengths and areas for improvement.
    * I can propose software and hardware modifications to improve the effectiveness and efficiency of a mechatronic and/or automated system.



Now that you have completed your final test, you need to evaluate your project. This will involve a peer evaluation of contribution and management and an individual evaluation in relation to requirements, project management and potential improvements.
