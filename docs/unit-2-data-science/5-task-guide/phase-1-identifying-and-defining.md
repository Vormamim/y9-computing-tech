??? note "Define Your Purpose"

    Before finding a dataset or collecting data, develop a hypothesis on a specific area you have chosen from your mindmap. For example:

    * *“Suburbs with more public transport options have lower car ownership rates.”*
    * *“Rainfall in NSW has become more extreme over the past 10 years.”*
    * *“Not enough houses are being built in Australia to allow first-home buyers into the market”*
    * *“Bushfires are getting more frequent and more intense in NSW as global temperatures rise”*
    * *“Supermarkets in Australia are taking advantage of inflation to overcharge on necessary goods”*
    * *“Students who get involved in extracurricular activities are happier at Gosford High School”*
    * *“Students who are active at lunchtime have an improved sense of wellbeing in Period 5”*
    * *"Students who travel a long distance to Gosford High are less likely to complete homework"*
