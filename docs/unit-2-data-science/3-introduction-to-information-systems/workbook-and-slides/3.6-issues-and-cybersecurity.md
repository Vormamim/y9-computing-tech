# 3.6 Issues and Cybersecurity

!!! info "Learning Intention"

    * Investigate issues with the use of data, including cyber safety, security, privacy and ethics
    * Investigate data collection and interpretation adhering to privacy and cybersecurity principles, including specify what data is collected, who owns it, and how it will be protected



!!! success "Success Criteria"

    * I can analyse issues with data use including cyber safety, security, privacy and ethics
    * I can explain how data is collected, who owns it, and how it will be protected
