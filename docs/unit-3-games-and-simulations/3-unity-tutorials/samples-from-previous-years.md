# Samples from Previous Years

These marked well in a previous year.

The Mouse Mayhem game went a bit further and included moving enemies - nifty, but not required.

Kirby Adventure and Harold's Hungry were quite high quality for the level of this task, and the character design for angel wings was quite elaborate.

Remember, this software is actually used in industry (Think Hollow Knight, Silksong, Ori and the Will of the Wisps, Untitled Goose Game), so it's quite complicated to even get to the point we're asking you to. In other words, we're expecting you to try and provide vibrant graphics and good level design with increasing difficulty, but not necessarily move too far past the tutorials
