# Ethical, Social and Legal Issues

!!! info "Learning Intention"

    Consider the social impacts and ethical and legal responsibilities in games or simulations

    Explore how games and simulations address environmental, lifestyle, societal and economic challenges including cyber safety


!!! success "Success Criteria"

    I can distinguish between social, ethical and legal issues in game design.

    I can discuss the potential impact of a game in a given scenario.

    I can outline the lifestyle and societal challenges which need to be considered when designing video games.


Game developers need to consider more than whether a game works technically. The decisions made during development can affect players, other people and society. Developers therefore need to consider ethical, social and legal issues when deciding what a game should contain, what information it should collect and how players should interact with it.

## Ethical issues

Ethical issues concern questions about what is responsible, fair or right. A game may technically be capable of doing something without that automatically meaning that it should do it.

For example, a developer could design a system that gives players increasingly valuable rewards when they continue playing for long periods. The developer should consider whether the design is encouraging healthy engagement or deliberately attempting to keep players playing for longer than they intended.

Fairness is another ethical consideration. If players compete against each other, developers need to think about whether the rules provide a reasonable opportunity for players to succeed. A game that gives certain players significant advantages because they have paid additional money may raise questions about fairness, particularly if the game presents itself as a competitive experience.

## Social issues

Social issues concern the effects that a game can have on individuals and groups. Online games can provide opportunities for cooperation and communication, but they can also expose players to bullying, harassment and inappropriate behaviour.

Developers can respond through features such as:

* Reporting
* Blocking
* Moderation
* Restrictions on communication

Games can also influence social attitudes through the characters, cultures and groups they represent, so developers should consider whether their representations reinforce harmful stereotypes or exclude particular groups.

## Legal issues

Legal issues are concerned with requirements established by law.

Copyright is particularly relevant to game development because games contain many creative works, including:

* Artwork
* Music
* Photographs
* Sound effects
* Software code

Developers cannot simply assume that material found online is free to copy. They need to understand ownership, licensing and permission before using another person's work.

## Privacy

Privacy is another important legal and ethical consideration. Games may collect information such as account details, gameplay information or other personal data.

Developers need to consider:

* Why the information is being collected
* Whether it is necessary
* How it will be protected
* What users should be told about its collection and use

Collecting information simply because it is technically possible may not be appropriate.

## Responding to scenarios

When responding to an ethical, social or legal scenario, students should move beyond simply naming the issue. A strong response identifies the issue, explains its potential impact and explains why the developer should be concerned about it.

For example, if a game collects unnecessary personal information:

* The issue is privacy.
* The impact could be increased risk to players if the information is exposed or misused.
* The developer should therefore minimise unnecessary data collection and protect information appropriately.

!!! info "These issues often overlap.&#x20;"

    A decision can be legal but still raise ethical questions, or a design decision can have both social and ethical consequences.


The important skill is to apply the concept to the particular game scenario rather than simply listing a definition.
