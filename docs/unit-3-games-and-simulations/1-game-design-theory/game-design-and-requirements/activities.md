# Activities

#### **Question 1**

**Distinguish** between functional and non-functional requirements for a video game. Use an example of each to support your response.

***

#### Question 2

A game is being developed for a school tablet program. One requirement states that “the game must allow students to save their progress.”

**Is this a functional or non-functional requirement? Justify your response.**

***

#### Question 3

A developer is designing a game for a local museum. The game will be played on touchscreen kiosks by visitors aged between 8 and 14. Players explore a virtual environment and complete challenges based on information about the museum's exhibits. The museum also requires the game to be accessible to visitors with colour-vision deficiencies.

**Identify one functional requirement and one non-functional requirement from the scenario. Explain why each is classified in this way.**
