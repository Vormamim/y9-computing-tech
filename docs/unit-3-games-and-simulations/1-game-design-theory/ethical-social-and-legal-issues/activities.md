#### Question 5

**Stimulus**

> A game development company is creating an online adventure game aimed at students aged 13–16. Players explore a large virtual world, complete missions and compete against other players.
>
> The game will be free to download, but players will be able to purchase cosmetic items such as character outfits and accessories. Some items will only be available for a limited time, and players will receive notifications when these items are about to disappear from the store.
>
> The game will also include an online communication system that allows players to send messages to other players. The development team has proposed automatically recording messages that are reported by players so they can be reviewed by moderators. Players will be able to block other users, but the development team has not yet decided whether players should be able to use the game without providing personal information.
>
> The company plans to collect information about how players use the game so that it can recommend content and display advertisements that are more relevant to individual players. The company believes that collecting more information will allow it to improve the game and increase its revenue.
>
> Some members of the development team have raised concerns about the impact of the game on younger players. They are particularly concerned about players feeling pressured to make purchases, spending excessive amounts of time playing, being exposed to inappropriate communication, and having their personal information collected.

**Question**

**Discuss the social, ethical and legal issues that the development team should consider before releasing the game.**

In your response, refer to specific features of the proposed game and explain the possible implications of these features for players and the development company.
