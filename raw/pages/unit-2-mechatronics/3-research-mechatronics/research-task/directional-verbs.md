# Directional Verbs

When responding to a question in any task at school, it is important to consider **how** to answer the question. Instead of just filling a box with as much **Google gobbledygook, waffling** and **repetitive drivel,** you need to address what the question is actually asking you.&#x20;

The best way to do this is to look for the **`directional verb.`**&#x20;

## What is that?

The **directional verb,** normally at the beginning of a question (or a sentence if the question is multiple sentences), tells you how you need to frame your answer. For instance, consider the following:

{% hint style="info" %}
**`Explain`** how **data privacy** is maintained and what measures are in place to protect user information.
{% endhint %}

In the above, the directional verb is **explain. NESA** (accreditation body for schools in NSW) has a [glossary of key words](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/hsc/student-guide/glossary), which defines "**explain**" as:

* Relate cause and effect.
* Make the relationships between things evident.
* Provide why and/or how.

This means that when the above question asks you to **explain** how data privacy is maintained, you not only need to describe data privacy principles, but need to **explain&#x20;*****`how`*** privacy is maintained and make the **relationship** between data privacy maintenance and the measures in place **clear.**

**In short, consider HOW and/or WHY something is the case, and make connections between HOW / WHY and the WHAT.**

## **Another Example**

{% hint style="info" %}
**`Describe`** the **mechatronic system’s components**, including:

* **Inputs** (sensors and data sources)
* **Storage methods**
* **Transmission processes**
* **Processing units**
* **Actuators and outputs**
  {% endhint %}

In the above, the directional verb is **describe. NESA's** [glossary of key words](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/hsc/student-guide/glossary) defines "**describe**" as:

* Provide characteristics and features.

This means that in the above, whilst you need to describe **WHAT** the mechatronic system's components are, **WHAT** purpose they serve and provide information about their features, you do **not** need to explain how or why they are used.&#x20;
