# Instructions

#### 1. Select a Mechatronic System

As a group, select a specific mechatronic system that captures your collective interest.&#x20;

Options include:

* Robotic surgical systems
* Autonomous vehicles
* Industrial automation
* Smart home appliances
* Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (Drones)
* Assistive Technologies
* Smart Manufacturing Systems
* Agricultural Automation

Your teacher **may request** that **every group** chooses a different topic.

Ensure the chosen system has significant real-world relevance. Look on the **next page** to find out more about each of the above systems.

#### 2. System Components Analysis

* **`Describe`** the **mechatronic system’s components**, including:
  * **Inputs** (sensors and data sources)
  * **Storage methods**
  * **Transmission processes**
  * **Processing units**
  * **Actuators and outputs**

#### 3. Functional and Non-Functional Requirements

* **`Explain`** the **functional requirements** necessary for the system to operate effectively and why they are necessary.
* **`Explain`** **non-functional requirements** and why they are necessary, such as:
  * Performance
  * Reliability
  * Usability
  * Maintainability
  * Security considerations

#### 4. Ethical, Social, and Legal Considerations

* **`Assess`** the **ethical concerns** surrounding the system, including potential risks and benefits.
* **`Assess`** the **social impact**, including effects on employment and accessibility.
* **`Assess` legal issues**, including regulations, standards, and compliance issues related to the system’s use.

#### 5. Impact Assessment

* **`Analyse`** the **impact** of the selected mechatronic system on society and various industries.
* **`Analyse`** the **impact** it has had on **processes, productivity, and overall quality of life**.
  * Use **statistics, case studies, and examples** to support your findings.

#### 6. Cybersecurity and Data Privacy

* **`Describe`** potential **cybersecurity threats** and vulnerabilities associated with the system.
* **`Explain`** how **data privacy** is maintained and what measures are in place to protect user information.

#### 7. Careers in Mechatronics and Robotics

* **`Describe`** career paths related to the design, development, and implementation of robotic components and mechatronic systems.
* **`Outline`** key **roles, skills, and qualifications** required in this field.
