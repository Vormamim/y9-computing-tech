### What is MicroPython?

MicroPython is a lean and efficient implementation of the Python 3 programming language optimised to run on microcontrollers and small embedded systems. It offers a subset of Python's capabilities, allowing you to leverage Python's user-friendly syntax and libraries in resource-constrained environments. MicroPython is widely used in education and hobbyist projects due to its simplicity, powerful features, and extensive support for a wide range of hardware.

***

### What is a Raspberry Pi PICO?

The Raspberry Pi PICO is a small, low-cost microcontroller board built on the Raspberry Pi RP2040 chip. It is designed for physical computing projects and features a dual-core ARM Cortex-M0+ processor, 264KB of RAM, and a wide range of input/output options. The PICO supports MicroPython and C/C++ programming, making it versatile for various DIY and educational applications, along with prototyping and development of embedded systems.
