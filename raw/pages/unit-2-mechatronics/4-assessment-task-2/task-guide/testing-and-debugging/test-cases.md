<summary>Adjust and Test your Code</summary>

Make adjustments to your code and continually test and improve **this specific section of the program** until you get it working as well as you possibly can.

Try not to get too worried about what work or won't work - just get stuck into it and experiment! Nothing happens if your attempts fail - **failure is celebrated, trial and error is encouraged!**

**Consider the following in your answer:**&#x20;

* How successful were you in meeting the test case requirements?
* What steps did you take to identify and fix errors?
* What went particularly well?
* What challenged you?
* What areas of your program could be improved based on the test results?
