# Task Guide

In order to complete your task, you will be working through a particular management approach. You will be working through **some of the software development steps,** in a **waterfall approach.** This means that for the most part, **one step must be finished before you move on to the next - each time we go down a waterfall, we cannot swim back up.**

Please look through these pages as you completed your assessment task in order to get a sense of **what** you need to do for each section in order to be successful.

{% hint style="warning" %}

### **Don't Overcook It!**

**Rememeber:** The goal is to complete all elements of the task, **not** to create the most complicated project to ever exist.&#x20;

Keep it simple, focus on the process rather than the final product.
{% endhint %}
