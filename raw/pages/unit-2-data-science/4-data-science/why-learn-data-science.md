# Why Learn Data Science?

{% hint style="info" %}

## Learning Intention

* Investigate issues with the use of data, including cyber safety, security, privacy and ethics
* Evaluate whether solutions meet social, ethical and legal responsibilities and cybersecurity principles
  {% endhint %}

{% hint style="success" %}

## Success Criteria

* I can consider the real-world implications of inaccurate data analysis.
* I can imagine a real-world scenario where inaccurate data analysis has led to disaster.
* I can discuss examples of social, ethical and legal issues associated with data.&#x20;
  {% endhint %}
