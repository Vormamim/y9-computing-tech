# Questions to Complete

1. Let's check how much a Big Mac has costed in Australia at two different points of time. Sort the data and create relevant formulas to assist you in this.

   1. Look at the **first** Australian entry. Is it **above** or **below** the average cost in USD at the time?
   2. Look at the **most recent** Australian entry. Is it above or below the average cost in USD at the time?
   3. Pick **one** in the middle (around 2012-2013). Is it above or below the average cost in USD at the time?
   4. Create **a chart** comparing the Australian cost of a Big Mac versus the average (in USD) at the above three times.
   5. **Discuss** the purchasing power of the Australian dollar over the last 25 years. Use **1-2 SEE paragraphs** to do this. Consider:
      1. Has it increased or decreased over time?
      2. How does it compare with other like countries (e.g. New Zealand, UK, France, US)
      3. Do you think the Australian dollar is better or worse off at current times.

2. Look back at the **original** dataset. Filter the data to show all years of Australia's data.
   1. Create a **line chart** showing the cost of Big Macs over time.&#x20;
   2. Can you **identify** any periods of time where you feel cost of living accelerated more quickly in Australia than others? **Explain** why you have selected these time periods.
