# What If...?

Choose one of the following 'What If' questions and **discuss** the question in **200-300 words.** You can support your answer with **Internet research.**

**Remember - SEE (State, Explain, Example)** for each paragraph (1-2 paragraphs is enough for this).&#x20;

* What if someone gained access to all of your online accounts?
* What if it was illegal to collect an individual’s data for any reason?
* What if private enterprise had to pay to collect individual's data?
* What if social media were a paid service instead of being free?
* What if social media companies could not collect personal use data for advertisers?
* What if Spotify could use any of your usage data?
* What if hospitals used ChatGPT to analyse all patient records to identify who needs further medical care?
* What if we did not collect any data on COVID-19 during the pandemic?
