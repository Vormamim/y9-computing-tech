# Imagine...

Imagine your own scenario where inaccurate data in a system has caused a newsworthy disaster.&#x20;

**Present your scenario using a medium of your choosing:**

{% hint style="info" %}

## Suggestions

* Write a news report (keep it looking evidence-based)
* Write an opinion piece (use LOTS of persuasive language here - feel free to make OUTRAGEOUS claims!)
* Film a news report or opinion piece
* Act out the scenario
* Compose (and possibly perform) a song about the scenario
* **These are only suggestions - it is up to you!**
  {% endhint %}
