# Course Information

The Computing Technology course, first introduced in 2024, represents a significant update to the curriculum, which had remained unchanged since 2003. This transformation brings a contemporary perspective to computing education, addressing the substantial evolution in the field over two decades. Students and educators are fortunate to engage with this modern syllabus, given that computing in 2003 would appear drastically different and outdated today.&#x20;

However, it's important to recognize that even as the syllabus was being finalized around 2022, the landscape of computing was rapidly shifting. Notably, advancements in generative AI, which were less developed and not widely utilized until the end of 2022, highlight the fast-paced nature of technological change. This context underscores the need for continuous adaptation and learning within the field of computing.
